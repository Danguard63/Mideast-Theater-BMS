Mideast for Falcon BMS 4.38

Before installing:

- Close Falcon BMS.
- Make a backup of your Falcon BMS installation (at least Data\Add-On Mideast128 if you already have an older Mideast128 theater).
- You need about 20 GB of free disk space during the installation.
- All the parts of the installer (Mideast-4.38-Setup.exe and the .bin files) must be in the same folder.

The installer:
- copies the theater into <Falcon BMS 4.38>\Data\Add-On Mideast128;
- deletes an older Mideast128 theater (4.36/4.37) in that folder, after asking you: it does not work with 4.38. Copy elsewhere anything you want to keep from it first;
- adds the theater to Data\TerrData\TheaterDefinition\theater.lst (a copy of the file is kept as theater.lst.before-mideast).

After installing, start BMS, choose "Mideast4_38" in the theater list and restart BMS.
