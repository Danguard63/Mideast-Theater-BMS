**Mideast for Falcon BMS 4.38 – New Terrain**, the Mideast128 theater by **SpbGoro**, brought to BMS 4.38 and the New Terrain with the [BMS Suite](https://github.com/Danguard63/BMS-suite).

## Download and install
1. Download **all the files** below (`Mideast-4.38-Setup.exe` and the `.bin` parts) into the **same folder**.
2. Back up your Falcon BMS installation.
3. Run `Mideast-4.38-Setup.exe`: it finds your BMS 4.38 folder, installs the theater into `Data\Add-On Mideast128` and adds it to `theater.lst`.
4. Start BMS, choose **Mideast4_38** in the theater list and restart BMS.

Free space needed: about 20 GB during installation, about 16 GB once installed.

**Note:** an older Mideast128 (4.36/4.37) in `Data\Add-On Mideast128` is deleted by the installer, after asking you, since it does not work with 4.38. Copy anything you want to keep from it first.

## What's new
- Real Copernicus elevation and Sentinel-2 photoreal imagery over the whole 2048 × 2048 km theater.
- 141 airbases checked, 117 moved onto their real runways, 51 rebuilt as complete 4.38 bases.
- Hundreds of objectives, bridges and coastal sites fixed by hand.
- SAMs, AAA and radars corrected to real-world ranges.
- Original campaigns and TEs updated to the new map.
